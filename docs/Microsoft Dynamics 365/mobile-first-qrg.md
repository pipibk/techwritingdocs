# Quick Reference Guide: D365 Field Service Mobile
Task: Navigating the Work Order Lifecycle (Onsite to Billing)
Target Audience: Field Service Technicians (Mobile) & Dispatchers (Desktop)

## The Goal
This process is a persona-based training solution designed to bridge the gap between D365 Field Service functionality and real-world revenue. This module ensures that every click—from a Dispatcher's intake to a Technician's signature—directly accelerates the billing cycle.

## The "Why" (Business Impact)
**Revenue Protection:** Prevents "data leakage" by ensuring all Products, Services, and Kits are captured onsite for 100% billing accuracy.

**Inventory Integrity:** Syncs real-time parts usage from the Mobile App to the back office.

**User Adoption:** Simplifies complex D365 workflows into "job-first" steps that reduce technician frustration.

This process shows beyond click-by-click "button training." It is a coordinated workflow that masters the critical hand-off between the office and the field. By focusing on Price List accuracy and Work Order closure protocols, this process reduces the time between "Job Done" and "Invoice Sent."

## Step 1: Arrival & Time Tracking

Accuracy here ensures the customer is billed correctly for travel and labor.

1. Open the App: Tap the Booking on your Home screen agenda.

2. Start Travel: Tap Status → Select Traveling.

3. Arrive Onsite: Upon arrival, change Status to In Progress.

:::tip
This transition automatically timestamps your "Start Time" for the back-office billing team.
:::

>💡 Pro Tip: This transition automatically timestamps your "Start Time" for the back-office billing team.

## Step 2: Execution & Parts
Recording parts and services as you go prevents forgotten items on the final invoice.

### Adding Products (Parts)

1. Navigate to the Products tab.

2. Tap + New Work Order Product.

3. Use the Barcode Scanner (top right) to scan the part or search by name.

4. Action: Set Line Status to Used.

5. Verify Warehouse: Ensure the "Main Truck" or "Onsite" warehouse is selected to keep inventory synced.

### Adding Services (Labor/Kits)

1. Navigate to the Services tab.

2. Tap + New Work Order Service.

3. Select the Labor type (e.g., HVAC Standard Repair).

4. Enter the Duration (e.g., 90 minutes).

Note: If using a Kit, all bundled items will prepopulate here.

## Step 3: Evidence & Documentation
Photos protect the company from liability and prove the work was done.

- Service Tasks: Go to the Service Tasks tab.

- Mark Complete: Tap the checkbox for each task finished.

- Attachments: Use the Camera Icon to take "Before" and "After" photos. These link directly to the Work Order in the cloud.

## Step 4: Customer Sign-off & Billing
The final step to trigger the invoice.

- Summarize: Use the Copilot Summary button to generate a quick text summary of the work performed.

- Signature: Tap the Notes tab → Select Signature.

- Capture: Have the customer sign on the device and tap Save.

- Complete: Change Booking Status to Completed.

> ### 💡 Note to the Trainer
> **Why this matters:** When a technician changes a status to **"Completed"** in the field, it triggers the Work Order Status to "Completed" on the desktop. This allows the Billing Clerk to generate an invoice against the correct **Price List** immediately, reducing the "Quote-to-Cash" cycle by up to **48 hours**.
